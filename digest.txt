1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW
================================================================================
KRISHIOS is an evidence-driven, unified digital agricultural operating system and
decision desk engineered for Maharashtra smallholders, Farmer Producer
Organizations (FPOs), agribusinesses, and agronomic researchers.

Solving from micro/nano ground operational challenges up to macro market linkage
and institutional access:
- Micro/Nano: 3-Phase rural electricity feeder schedules, emergency snakebite &
  pesticide poisoning first-aid triage with Anti-Snake Venom (ASV) hospital locator,
  and zero-cost university DIY desi bio-inputs (Jeevamrut, Dashparni Ark).
- Meso/Operations: Farm labor (Krishi Majur) wage matching and labor gang directory,
  spurious seed/pesticide lot authenticity verification, and groundwater depth
  monitoring with aquifer recharge pit advisory.
- Macro/Market Linkage: Direct farm-to-buyer Kisan Bazaar (0% APMC commission) and
  PMFME micro food-processing profiles with 35% government capital subsidies.
- Institutional & Access: RBI/NABARD 4% subsidized KCC loan dossiers, Kisan Nyay
  legal dispute resolution (Mamlatdar Courts Act Sec 143 for farm roads), and
  omnichannel IVR missed-call & WhatsApp bot gateways for non-smartphone farmers.

Core Technical Stack:
- Backend: FastAPI 0.115+, Python 3.11+, SQLAlchemy 2.0+, Pydantic v2, Argon2
- Frontend: Next.js 14, React 18, TypeScript, Vanilla CSS (Dual-Theme System)
- Data Integrations: Open-Meteo, IMD, MSAMB APMC, ICAR, MSEDCL, CGWB/GSDA, PMFME
- Data Provenance Framework: Strict enforcement of zero hallucination. Every
  recommendation is stamped LIVE, HISTORICAL, ESTIMATED, USER_PROVIDED, or UNAVAILABLE.

Dual-Theme Architecture & Rural Accessibility:
- Theme 1: "🌿 Dribbble Modern Precision AgTech" (Dark forest #08160c, lime #d4f936)
- Theme 2: "🏛️ Official Govt AgriStack Portal" (Light high-contrast e-Governance)
- 🌾 Easy Kisan Mode (सुलभ शेतकरी मोड): 12 one-tap cards with Devanagari TTS audio.

3. KEY DOMAIN ALGORITHMS & PROVENANCE LAWS
================================================================================

3.1 3-Phase Rural Electricity Feeder Roster
- 8-Hour Rotational Cycles: Day Shift (08:00 to 16:00) vs. Night Shift (23:00 to 07:00).
- Voltage Quality Health Check: Monitors 3-phase steady state (415V optimal,
  <380V low voltage alert with single phase preventer and shunt capacitor rules).

3.2 Kisan Emergency SOS & ASV Hospital Locator
- Golden Hour 60-Minute Triage: Immediate immobilization, no tourniquets, no incision.
- ASV Inventory: District Civil Hospital, Sub-District Hospital, and Rural PHC stock
  tracking with direct call to 108 Ambulance and 104 Health Information Helpline.
- Organophosphate Toxicity Protocol: Decontamination, skin wash, emergency atropine.

3.3 Zero-Cost Desi Bio-Inputs ("Ghar-cha-Doctor")
- Formulations: Jeevamrut, Dashparni Ark, Sour Buttermilk (Khati Chhach), Neemastra.
- Cost Guarantee: ₹0 to ₹50/acre using farm-available cow dung, cow urine, besan, jaggery.
- Economic Benefit: Saves ₹2,500/acre over synthetic chemical inputs.

3.4 Krishi Majur Labor Wage Matching Hub
- Prevailing Benchmarks: Standard 8-hour daily wage rates and piece-rate benchmarks
  for cotton picking, soybean harvesting, and onion transplanting.
- Crew Registry: Contactable labor toli mukadams with member count and availability.

3.5 Spurious Seed & Input Fraud Verification
- Lot Format Verification: Validates format against Mahabeej / National Seed Corp norms.
- Notified Pricing & Germination: Checks minimum statutory germination % (70-75%) and
  notified maximum retail price (MRP) with direct link to Quality Control Inspector.

3.6 Groundwater Depth & Aquifer Recharge Design
- CGWB/GSDA Basalt Classification: Depth below ground level (pre vs. post monsoon).
- Safe Drilling Limit: Flags 300ft basalt fracture risk to prevent dry borewell loss.
- 4-Layer Recharge Pit Design: Sand (6"), Gravel (12"), Boulders (18"), SS mesh casing.

3.7 Direct Kisan Bazaar & PMFME 35% Processing
- Farm-Gate Direct Lots: 0% APMC commission with direct telephone and WhatsApp link.
- PMFME Value-Add Units: Mini Dal Mills, Lakadi Ghani oil expeller, solar onion
  dehydration with 35% capital subsidy (up to ₹10 Lakhs) and 10% farmer margin.

3.8 KCC Loan Dossier & Kisan Nyay Legal Aid
- Scale of Finance: DLTC district rates (e.g. ₹28,000/acre for Soybean, ₹36,000 for Cotton).
- Composite Credit Limit: Base crop loan + 10% post-harvest/household + 20% maintenance.
- Interest Subvention: 7% base minus 3% timely repayment = 0% to 4% effective interest.
- Kisan Nyay: Mamlatdar Courts Act Sec 143 for farm roads; Consumer Protection Act for seeds.
