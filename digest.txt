1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW
================================================================================
KRISHIOS is an evidence-driven, unified digital agricultural operating system and
decision desk engineered for Maharashtra smallholders, Farmer Producer
Organizations (FPOs), agribusinesses, and agronomic researchers.

Solving from micro/nano ground operational challenges up to macro market linkage
and institutional access:
- Micro/Nano: 3-Phase rural electricity feeder schedules, emergency snakebite &
  pesticide poisoning first-aid triage with sample hospital directory (no drug dosages),
  and university DIY desi bio-inputs (Jeevamrut, Dashparni Ark).
- Meso/Operations: Farm labor (Krishi Majur) wage matching and labor gang directory,
  seed lot format validation, and groundwater depth monitoring with aquifer recharge pit advisory.
- Macro/Market Linkage: Direct farm-to-buyer Kisan Bazaar (simulated demo) and
  PMFME micro food-processing profiles with 35% government capital subsidies.
- Institutional & Access: RBI/NABARD KCC loan dossiers (Maharashtra 0% interest subvention),
  Kisan Nyay legal dispute resolution (Mamlatdar Courts Act Sec 143 for farm roads), and
  omnichannel IVR missed-call & WhatsApp bot gateways for non-smartphone farmers.

Core Technical Stack:
- Backend: FastAPI 0.115+, Python 3.11+, SQLAlchemy 2.0+, Pydantic v2, Argon2
- Frontend: Next.js 14, React 18, TypeScript, Vanilla CSS (Dual-Theme System)
- Data Integrations: Open-Meteo, MSAMB APMC, ICAR, MSEDCL, PMFME, WDRA
- Data Provenance Framework: Strict enforcement of data integrity. Every
  recommendation is stamped LIVE, HISTORICAL, CURATED_REFERENCE, ESTIMATED, SIMULATED,
  USER_PROVIDED, or UNAVAILABLE.

Dual-Theme Architecture & Rural Accessibility:
- Theme 1: "🌿 Dribbble Modern Precision AgTech" (Dark forest #08160c, lime #d4f936)
- Theme 2: "🏛️ Official Govt AgriStack Portal" (Light high-contrast e-Governance)
- 🌾 Easy Kisan Mode (सुलभ शेतकरी मोड): Exactly 8 one-tap cards with Devanagari TTS audio.

3. KEY DOMAIN ALGORITHMS & PROVENANCE LAWS
================================================================================

3.1 3-Phase Rural Electricity Feeder Roster
- 8-Hour Rotational Cycles: Day Shift (08:00 AM to 04:00 PM) vs. Night Shift (11:00 PM to 07:00 AM).
- Voltage Quality Health Check: Monitors 3-phase steady state (415V optimal,
  <380V low voltage alert with single phase preventer and shunt capacitor rules).
- Feeder Schedule Note: Sample data - Schedules vary by feeder. Confirm with your local MSEDCL office.

3.2 Kisan Emergency SOS & Hospital Locator
- Golden Hour 60-Minute Triage: Immediate immobilization, no tourniquets, no incision, call 108.
- Sample Directory: Sample hospital directory labelled to call and confirm Anti-Snake Venom (ASV)
  availability prior to transport. Zero numeric stock counts or unverified drug dosages.
- Pesticide Toxicity Protocol: Immediate decontamination, skin wash, take chemical container to hospital.

3.3 University Desi Bio-Inputs
- Formulations: Jeevamrut, Dashparni Ark, Sour Buttermilk (Khati Chhach), Neemastra.
- Cost Guarantee: Low-cost traditional organic preparations using farm-available cow dung, urine, besan, jaggery.

3.4 Krishi Majur Labor Wage Matching Hub
- Prevailing Benchmarks: Standard 8-hour daily wage rates and piece-rate benchmarks
  for cotton picking, soybean harvesting, and onion transplanting (Sample data, UNREVIEWED).
- Crew Directory: Contactable labor toli mukadams with member count and availability.

3.5 Seed Format Checker
- Lot Format Verification: Format syntax check against Indian seed lot naming conventions.
  Note: This is a format check only. It does NOT confirm the seed is genuine.

3.6 Groundwater Depth & Aquifer Advisory
- GSDA Basalt Classification: Depth below ground level (pre vs. post monsoon).
- Safe Drilling Limit: Flags basalt fracture risks to prevent dry borewell loss.
- 4-Layer Recharge Pit Design: Sand (6"), Gravel (12"), Boulders (18"), SS mesh casing.

3.7 Direct Kisan Bazaar & PMFME 35% Processing
- Farm-Gate Direct Lots: Simulated demo community marketplace.
- PMFME Value-Add Units: Mini Dal Mills, Lakadi Ghani oil expeller, solar onion
  dehydration with 35% capital subsidy calculations.

3.8 KCC Loan Dossier & Kisan Nyay Legal Aid
- Scale of Finance: DLTC district rates (e.g. ₹28,000/acre for Soybean, ₹36,000 for Cotton).
- Interest Subvention: 0% net farmer interest in Maharashtra up to ₹3 Lakhs under prompt repayment.
- Kisan Nyay: Mamlatdar Courts Act Sec 143 for farm roads; Consumer Protection Act for seeds.
