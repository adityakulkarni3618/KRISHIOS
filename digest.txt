1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW
================================================================================
KRISHIOS is an evidence-driven digital agricultural decision desk engineered for
Maharashtra cultivators, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and agronomists.
The platform bridges raw agronomic data and actionable on-field recommendations
with mathematical precision, zero data fabrication, and native multilingual
support (Marathi, Hindi, English).

Key Architecture:
- Backend: FastAPI 0.115+, Python 3.11+, SQLAlchemy 2.0+, Pydantic v2, Argon2
- Frontend: Next.js 14, React 18, TypeScript, Vanilla CSS (Dual-Theme System)
- Data Integrations: Open-Meteo, IMD, MSAMB APMC, ICAR agronomic standards
- Storage: SQLite for development, extensible to PostgreSQL / PostGIS
- Dual-Theme System:
  * Theme 1: "Dribbble Precision AgTech" (Dark forest #08160c, lime #d4f936)
  * Theme 2: "Official Govt AgriStack Portal" (Govt of Maharashtra e-Governance)
  * Seamless instant toggle via [ 🌿 | 🏛️ ] or clicking the brand logo.

3. DATA PROVENANCE SPECIFICATION (AGENTS.md)
================================================================================
To eliminate hallucination and uphold strict agricultural safety, every metric
in KRISHIOS is stamped with an explicit provenance value:

1. LIVE:
   Directly fetched from active upstream APIs (Open-Meteo, MSAMB mandi arrivals)
   within an active cache TTL.
2. HISTORICAL:
   Verified published government archives (e.g., scheme eligibility criteria,
   standard university baseline yields).
3. ESTIMATED:
   Deterministically calculated by transparent arithmetic models (e.g. farm
   economics break-even analysis, sensitivity variations).
4. USER_PROVIDED:
   Values entered directly by the farmer or soil testing laboratory (e.g.
   pH readings, farm parcel acreage).
5. UNAVAILABLE:
   Returned when upstream services fail or data does not exist. Never replaced
   with fabricated or synthetic fallback data.

4. KEY DOMAIN ALGORITHMS & FORMULAS
================================================================================

4.1 Farm Economics & Break-Even Engine
- Total Cost (C_total) = C_seed + C_fert + C_pest + C_labor + C_mach + C_irrig + C_other
- Cost per Hectare = C_total / Area_ha
- Total Production (Y_total) = Yield_per_ha * Area_ha
- Gross Revenue (R) = Y_total * Price_per_quintal
- Net Profit (P) = R - C_total
- Return on Investment (ROI %) = (P / C_total) * 100
- Break-Even Price (P_be) = C_total / Y_total
- Break-Even Yield (Y_be) = (C_total / Price_per_quintal) / Area_ha
- 5x5 Sensitivity Matrix:
  Computes Net Profit across 25 variations:
  Yield multipliers: [-20%, -10%, 0%, +10%, +20%]
  Price multipliers: [-20%, -10%, 0%, +10%, +20%]

4.2 ICAR Soil Health Interpretation Rules
- Soil Reaction (pH):
  * pH < 6.5: Strongly / Moderately Acidic
    Recommendation: Apply agricultural lime (CaCO3) or dolomite @ 2-4 t/ha
    before sowing; avoid acid-forming fertilizers (e.g. Ammonium Sulphate).
  * 6.5 <= pH <= 7.8: Optimal / Neutral
    Recommendation: Soil reaction is ideal for crop nutrient availability.
    Maintain organic carbon with balanced N-P-K applications.
  * pH > 7.8: Moderately / Strongly Alkaline (Calcareous)
    Recommendation: Apply agricultural gypsum (CaSO4.2H2O) @ 2.5-5.0 t/ha;
    incorporate Farm Yard Manure (FYM) or green manure (Dhaincha/Sunnhemp)
    to improve soil microbial activity and micronutrient availability (Zn, Fe).
- Organic Carbon (OC %):
  * OC < 0.50%: Low
  * 0.50% <= OC <= 0.75%: Medium
  * OC > 0.75%: High

4.3 Agro-Meteorology & Spray Advisory Logic
- Spray Window: Wind speed < 12 km/h AND precipitation probability < 20%
- Heat Stress Warning: Ambient temperature >= 35.0°C (recommend evening drip irrigation)
- Inversion Alert: Wind speed < 3 km/h combined with high relative humidity (> 85%)
